# Speech fixtures

These three Mandarin human-read clips and their transcripts are from **Google FLEURS**,
`cmn_hans_cn`, validation rows 0, 1 and 2 (dataset IDs 1579, 1605, 1581).

- Dataset creators: Google and the FLEURS authors (Conneau et al., 2022).
- Source and dataset card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12446
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Revision: `70bb2e84b976b7e960aa89f1c648e09c59f894dd`.
- Retrieved 2026-09-27 from the Hugging Face dataset viewer. Full clips were converted
  from float WAV to 16-bit mono 16 kHz WAV using PyAV; no cuts or speech edits.
  Source and converted SHA-256 values are in `../speech.json`.

The clips and transcripts retain their CC BY 4.0 license; the repository's MIT
software license does not replace it. No endorsement is implied. These are read
speech, not spontaneous secretary requests, and do not establish conversational
accuracy or room echo performance. No private user recording is included.
